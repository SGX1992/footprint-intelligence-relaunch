Questions buyers ask

Straight answers, also for your AI assistant.

These are the questions we hear most, written so that a person or an AI search can quote them.

How can sustainability data support business goals like sales or margins?

Product carbon footprints and LCAs answer customer questionnaires and tenders that increasingly decide supplier selection. A corporate footprint broken down by site and energy source shows where cost and emissions fall together, so reduction measures can be ranked by euros saved. Climate risk and green claims data prevent losses before they occur. Footprint Intelligence links each data set to the goal it serves.

Can I use Footprint Intelligence data in ChatGPT, Claude or Microsoft Copilot?

Yes. Footprint Intelligence provides an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, so an AI assistant can query your footprint data, factors and calculations directly with the audit trail attached. The same data is available to BI tools and other software through the REST API. The MCP server is in early access.

What does the climate risk analysis cover?

Physical risks such as flooding, heat and water stress per site and supplier location, and transition risks such as carbon pricing and regulation per business line. Results are mapped to revenue and asset value and formatted for CSRD (ESRS E1), banks and insurers.

What is EmpCo, and what does the Green Claims Check do?

The EU Empowering Consumers Directive (EmpCo) restricts generic environmental claims such as "climate neutral" and requires evidence for the rest. The Green Claims Check reviews every public claim against those rules, links the supporting data and documents the review, so marketing, legal and sustainability sign off on the same evidence.

Does Footprint Intelligence replace our existing carbon or ESG tool?

It can, but it does not have to. Many customers connect existing tools through the API and use Footprint Intelligence as the calculation and intelligence layer on top. Others move their CCF, PCF and reporting fully into the platform. Both paths keep one audit trail.

What does it cost?

Pricing is modular: a platform base fee plus the modules you use, such as PCF, climate risk, Green Claims Check or API and MCP access. Platform base fee from €490 per month, modules from €190 per month each. API and MCP access from the Professional tier. The strategy call ends with a concrete recommendation for your setup.
